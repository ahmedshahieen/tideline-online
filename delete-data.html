Delete your data

Tideline Online for Android · Last updated 26 November 2026

In short

  • Tideline Online has no accounts. Almost everything it knows about you is stored on your own device, and you can delete it yourself in under a minute.
  • If you have played over the internet, Unity’s online services also hold a random guest identifier for your device. You can ask us to have it erased.

1. Delete everything on your device

This removes your callsign, career, coins, weapons, settings and the sign-in key of the guest identifier.

  1. Open your device’s Settings and go to Apps.
  2. Choose Tideline Online, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (on some devices Clear data) and confirm.

Uninstalling the game does the same. This cannot be undone: the career is not saved anywhere else.

Once the sign-in key is gone, the guest identifier can never be used again and nothing on your device points to it.

2. Ask for the guest identifier to be erased

Write to us from any email address and ask for your Tideline Online data to be deleted.

Email a deletion request

or write to ahmedwaliednoaah@gmail.com with the subject “Tideline Online: delete my data”.

Please include:

  • your Online ID. Open the game, go to Settings, then Privacy, and tap Copy beside it. It appears once you have played over the internet;
  • if you have already removed the game, your callsign and the date and approximate time of your last online match instead.

Why we ask for this. The guest identifier is a random code. It is not stored together with your name, your email address or your callsign, so we cannot look it up from those: the Online ID is what lets us find the right record. If you cannot send it and we cannot tell which identifier is yours, we will say so in our reply. In that case step 1 is the complete answer: it destroys the only key to the identifier, and the leftover code cannot be connected to you by anyone.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: the guest player record that Unity’s Authentication service holds for your device.
  • Kept for a short time: connection and room records in the logs of Unity’s services, which Unity states it removes within about 30 days. We cannot delete individual lines from those logs.
  • Not held at all: match data. It is not stored once a match has ended.

We act on a request within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done. There is no charge.
